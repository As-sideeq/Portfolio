About this project

Matthias at Huis De Waele, a Belgium-based fine jewellery atelier, needed a way for customers to design and customise bespoke rings online before committing to a commission. The traditional approach of email back-and-forth with sketches was slow and costly — leading to misunderstandings that resulted in expensive revisions after production had already started.

OTG Tech Hub built a sophisticated real-time 3D ring configurator using Next.js, TypeScript, and Three.js with photorealistic PBR materials. Customers can select stone shape, stone size, metal colour, band style, side stones, and halo options — with the ring updating live in 3D as they design. The configurator is embedded seamlessly into the Huis De Waele website, giving customers a true-to-life preview of their bespoke ring before a single stone is set.

The result was a dramatic reduction in design revision requests, a significant increase in bespoke order conversions, and a customer experience that now sets Huis De Waele apart from every competitor in the Belgian fine jewellery market.

What we built

Key features

Bespoke Ring Design

Customers select stone shape, size, metal colour, band style, side stones, and halo options to design their unique ring from scratch.

Photorealistic PBR Rendering

Physically Based Rendering ensures gold, platinum, and gemstones look true-to-life — customers see exactly what they'll receive.

360° Ring View

Customers can rotate and zoom the ring in full 360° to inspect every detail — the band, the setting, and the stone from every angle.

Next.js Performance

Built with Next.js and TypeScript for fast page loads, server-side rendering, and a smooth, responsive experience across all devices.

Mobile Optimised

Fully responsive and touch-friendly — the ring builder works smoothly on mobile and tablet with pinch-to-zoom and swipe controls.

Design Sharing

Customers can share their ring design with a unique link — making it easy to get a second opinion or discuss with their partner before ordering.
