About us

We turn products into experiences

Five years building real-time 3D configurators for brands that understand the power of letting customers see, touch, and customise before they buy.

Our story

OTG Tech Hub was founded with a singular mission: to close the gap between digital shopping and the tactile, immersive experience of seeing a product in person. We've spent over five years crafting real-time 3D configurators that give customers the confidence to buy.

From global sneaker brands to industrial manufacturers, we've partnered with businesses that understand the cost of a hesitant customer — and the power of letting them explore, customise, and fall in love with a product before it ships.

What makes us different

We don't just build 3D viewers — we engineer buying moments. Every configurator is optimised for conversion, engineered for performance, and designed to feel native to your brand.

Our team combines deep expertise in WebGL, Three.js, and AR development with a genuine understanding of e-commerce conversion mechanics. We've studied what works — and what wastes load time.

  • Performance-first: sub-3s load times on mobile
  • No plugins required — pure WebGL in the browser
  • AR-ready from day one, built to future-proof your stack
  • End-to-end delivery: modelling, dev, and integration
